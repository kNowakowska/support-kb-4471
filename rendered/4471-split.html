The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a day of 12 or less swaps with the month: 7 April becomes 4 July. This reaches us through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. It is the bug #4471 named, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first and reads the day first. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, but the parser is not involved. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 have it. It needs a change to the form.

move: the date moves by one day. These are not swaps: Tuesday becomes Monday, a meeting lands on two days in Lisbon and Toronto, an invite arrives before its booking. The desk already read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but they were filed under parser by tenant. This looks like timezone or DST handling, so it needs its own investigation.

How this was counted

  • The export repeats some reports, because its pages overlap. One id is one report.
  • A report goes under parser or hint by its tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), and under move when the desk read it as a one-day move. Each report is counted once.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. Reporters with an en-US browser were tagged hint whatever their tenant, so the tag does not decide anything here. The table shows it only for comparison.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line shows that reports look the same, not that they have the same cause. Split a ticket as soon as its reports point at different fixes.
  • Check a “known” ticket against its data before calling it one bug.
